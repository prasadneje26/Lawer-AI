LAWER-AI 2.0 – COMPLETE PROFESSIONAL LEGAL AI ASSISTANT
Project Objective

Build a production-ready AI-powered Legal Assistant platform named Lawer-AI that assists lawyers, legal researchers, law firms, and law students in legal research, document analysis, case retrieval, legal drafting, and intelligent decision support.

The system must be reliable, scalable, secure, responsive, and visually professional. The final product should look like software actually used inside a law firm, court office, or legal consultancy.
